Three tools in one place. Each keeps its own sheets and marks in this browser, and switching tabs never loses your place.

URL Checker

Upload your sheets, paste a LinkedIn URL and see straight away whether it is already in one, with the file and row.

Calibrate

Decide Yes or No on profiles and see whether you matched the team's decision, with the comment behind every no.

Review

Approved Feed

Go through every approved profile from your other projects with the team's comments, keep the useful ones, restart any time.

Your data stays in this browser. Clearing site data removes it, so export from each tool before you do.
